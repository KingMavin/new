# Privacy Policy — Immortal Path

**Last updated:** with release v0.16.0

Immortal Path is a single-player idle cultivation RPG. This policy explains
what data the game handles. Short version: **the game collects nothing.**

## What the game stores and where

Your progress (cultivation, chronicle, deeds, settings) is saved **on your
device only**, under the app's own local storage (a single save record in
`shared_preferences`). This save:

- never leaves your device unless *you* copy it yourself using the
  **Export save** button (which places the save text on your clipboard)
- is not uploaded to any server, because the game has no servers

## What the game does not do

- No account or sign-in of any kind
- No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting
- No advertising SDKs (the Temple of Support's optional "blessings" are
  simulated by a stub in this build)
- No network access for gameplay — the game runs fully offline

## In-app purchases

If a future build enables real store billing, purchases will be processed
by Google Play / the App Store under their own privacy policies; the game
itself will continue to store purchase state only on your device. Any such
change will be reflected in this policy before release.

## Data removal

You can erase your entire save at any time via **Settings → Erase & start
anew**, or by uninstalling the app, which removes all local data.
